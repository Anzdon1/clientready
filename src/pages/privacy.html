Privacy

This site exists to find out whether ClientReady is worth building. Here is exactly what it collects.

Your email address

When you submit the early-access form, your email, the page you submitted from, and any campaign tags in the link you arrived on (for example utm_source=reddit) are sent to Formspree, which delivers them to the ClientReady team. We use the address for one purpose: to tell you when there is something to try. We do not sell it or share it with anyone else. Email hello@clientready.cc to have it removed.

Analytics

Pages use Google Analytics 4 to count visits, see which page people arrive on, and record when a signup completes. We do not send your email address or anything you type into the form to Analytics. Your browser's “do not track” setting is respected by Google's tag where the browser exposes it.

Cookies and storage

Google Analytics sets its usual first-party cookies. The site itself stores one item in your browser for the current tab session: the campaign tags from the link you arrived on, so a signup from the homepage can still be credited to the post that brought you here. It is cleared when the tab closes.

Who we are

ClientReady is an early-stage student project from Stark Labs, built in Columbia University's Startup Studio course (COMS 4995, Fall 2026). Work in the course is public by policy. The sample projects shown on this site are invented examples, not real customers.
