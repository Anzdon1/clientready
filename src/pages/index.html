What it does, and what it doesn't.

Most tools in this space promise to make people respond. We promise something smaller and keep it.

  • Starts from the material you already have. Paste the document, add the image folder, list the pages you agreed on. That's the whole setup.
  • Asks short questions, only where something is missing. Each one names the page and section it is for, and shows an example of a useful answer.
  • Hands you content organized by page. Section text, the image that goes with it, where it came from, and what is still undecided.
  • Keeps people in control. The contributor confirms the meaning; you decide what is usable. A file arriving is not approval, and neither is silence.

Where it stops

If a shared document already works for your team, keep it. ClientReady is meant to live alongside the doc first and earn its place.

It does not write your business for you, and it does not make an unresponsive person respond. It organizes what exists and names what doesn't.
