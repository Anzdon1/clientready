What it does, and what it doesn't

  • Starts from the material you already have. A document, a folder of images, a chat thread. No one has to retype anything into a new tool first.
  • Asks short questions, only where something is missing. Each question is tied to a page and a section, with an example of how much detail is useful.
  • Hands you content organized by page. Section text, the image that goes with it, where it came from, and what is still undecided.
  • Keeps people in control. Suggested placement is a suggestion. The contributor confirms the meaning; you decide what is usable. Silence is never treated as approval.

Honest limits

If a shared document already works for your team, keep it. ClientReady is meant to live alongside the doc first and earn its place.

It does not write your business for you, and it does not make an unresponsive person respond. It organizes what exists and names what doesn't.
